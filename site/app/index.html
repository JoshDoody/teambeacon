Load Employee Data

Provide a spreadsheet with columns: Name, Performance, Potential, Risk of Loss, Impact of Loss. Values must be 1, 2, or 3.

Start from a Google Sheet with your employee list. The only required column is Name; you can also include any or all of Performance, Potential, Risk of Loss, and Impact of Loss (values 1–3). Missing ratings default to 2.

To load your sheet: File → Share → Publish to web → choose the sheet tab → select Comma-separated values (.csv) → click Publish. Paste the link below.

When you're done, use Export CSV to save your work and bring it back into Google Sheets.
